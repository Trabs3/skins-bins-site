About us

We built this because we were tired of bad data.

The skin market moves fast. Prices shift by the hour, platforms disagree on values, and most tools are either outdated, paywalled, or just wrong.

Skin Beans started as an internal tool — a simple script that pulled prices from multiple sources and compared them. We used it ourselves. Then we showed it to a few friends. Then a few more.

Now we're building it properly. Clean interface, reliable data, no subscription required to see basic prices.

What we believe
01

Data should be honest

We show raw numbers. No artificial smoothing, no "estimated" prices that don't match reality.

02

Speed matters

Skin prices change fast. Our data refreshes every 15 minutes. Not daily, not hourly — 15 minutes.

03

Simple by default

You shouldn't need a tutorial to check a price. We keep the interface minimal on purpose.

We're in early access. Small group, real feedback.
